# Staff guide — people, learners and statistics

This guide is for staff of DIESIS, Pro Arbeit, Synthesis and University of Macedonia. This is version 3. It describes the staff areas as they work from **Friday 20 November 2026** (Release 3), before the pilot starts on 1 December. What changed on 10 November (Release 2) and on 20 November (Release 3) is summed up at the end. Where Release 2 changed what a screen shows, the text says **From 10 November**. How to build courses is in the *Lesson editor partner guide*. How data requests are handled is in the *Data management guide* (`docs/data-management.md`).

## Sign in

There are no passwords. You sign in with a link sent to your email.

1. Go to **seq-elevate.eu** and press **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email address and press **Send me a link**.
3. In the email, press **Sign in**. The link works once, within 24 hours.

An invitation email has a **Join SEQ Elevate** button that signs you in directly. It works once, within 7 days. After that, sign in as above.

- Always use the address your admin added. Any other address creates a separate learner account.
- You stay signed in on that browser until you press **Sign out**, or until 30 days pass without a visit. On a shared computer, always sign out.

### The first time: "Before you start"

The first time you sign in from 20 November, you see **Before you start**. Everyone answers it once, staff too: your first name or nickname, the country you take part from, the Terms of use, and your age. Then you go on to your workspaces.

- Your country doesn't change your organisation, or whose learners you see. DIESIS sets your organisation.
- When the Terms of use change, you are asked to accept them again.
- You can't change your name or country afterwards. Ask DIESIS (see *A learner asks to change their name or country*; the same applies to staff).

## Your workspaces

After you sign in, the **Welcome back** page shows a card for each area you can use:

| Role | Cards you see |
|---|---|
| Learner | None: you go straight to **My learning** |
| Editor | Create courses · Manage courses · My learning |
| Facilitator | Support learners · Statistics · My learning |
| Partner admin (includes Editor and Facilitator) | Create courses · Manage courses · Manage people · Support learners · Statistics · My learning |
| Admin (includes Editor) | Create courses · Manage courses · Manage people · Support learners · Statistics · My learning |

With two roles you see both sets. The menu in the top bar switches workspace. Role changes show on your next page.

## Whose learners you see

From 20 November, each partner sees only its own learners.

- **Admins** (DIESIS, and SENIC for support) see every learner in the project, including learners who have no organisation yet.
- **Partner admins and facilitators** see the learners of their own organisation: all its cohorts. A DIESIS facilitator sees DIESIS's learners (Belgium and other countries).
- **Editors** see no learner data, only how many learners a course has.
- **Staff without an organisation** see no learners. The screen says: "Your account isn't linked to an organisation yet, so no learners are shown. Ask DIESIS to link it."

**A learner's organisation** comes from the country they give: Germany → Pro Arbeit, Cyprus → Synthesis, Greece → University of Macedonia, Belgium and any other country → DIESIS. A learner your organisation adds belongs to your organisation. Only an admin changes a learner's organisation.

**When partner staff start.** Partner staff see learners only after a security check of this rule on the live site. The check starts on Monday 23 November with test accounts. If it passes, DIESIS links real staff to their organisations and partner admins are invited from Wednesday 25 November.

## The partner admin role

Each partner organisation gets a **Partner admin**. Only an admin (DIESIS or SENIC) gives this role.

A partner admin can:
- do everything an editor does: create, edit and translate courses;
- support their organisation's learners, like a facilitator (**Support learners**, **Statistics**);
- manage their organisation's people on **Manage people**: add and invite learners and facilitators, switch the **Learner** and **Facilitator** roles, put people in their organisation's cohorts, and remove people;
- correct a learner's name (**Change details**);
- record that a parent's or guardian's permission is held for a learner under 16 they invite;
- switch German or Greek on for a course once its translation is checked (*German and Greek*, below).

A partner admin can't:
- give the **Editor**, **Partner admin** or **Admin** role;
- change or remove someone who holds another role (an editor, another partner admin), or see admins;
- change anyone's country or organisation, or add someone who already belongs to another organisation;
- create or delete organisations, or change which country goes to which organisation;
- use the **Data** page, mark test accounts, or edit the safeguarding contacts.

When the screen says "Ask DIESIS", an admin has to do it.

## Manage people

**Manage people** opens the overview.

- **For an admin**, the **Admin dashboard** shows four figures: **Learners** (staff, former staff and test accounts left out), **Cohorts**, **Organisations** and **Published courses**. Below them are **Organisations & cohorts**, **People & roles**, **Data** and **Safeguarding contacts**.
- **For a partner admin**, the page is called **‹your organisation› overview**. It shows **Learners at your organisation** and **Your organisation's cohorts**, and links to **Cohorts** and **People & roles**.

These screens are in English only.

### Organisations, countries and cohorts

**Admins**, on **Organisations & cohorts**:

1. Under **New organisation**, enter the **Name** and, if you like, a **Short name**. Press **Add organisation**.
2. Under **Country mapping**, choose the organisation that supports learners from each country and press **Save**. A learner joins the organisation of the country they give, the first time they finish **Before you start**. Tick **Also place its learners who have no organisation yet** to give the organisation that country's learners who have none. Until a country is mapped, only DIESIS and SENIC see its learners.
3. In an organisation's box, enter a **New cohort** name and, if you like, **Starts** and **Ends**. Press **Add cohort**.

**Partner admins** see the same page as **Cohorts**, with their own organisation only. They add and delete their own cohorts.

- The dates are for information only. Nothing happens when a cohort ends.
- You can't change names or dates yet. Ask SENIC.
- The bin icons ask first and say what will happen, with the number of people in the cohort. Press **Delete cohort** or **Delete organisation** to go ahead, or **Keep it**. Once you have pressed Delete, it can't be stopped. If you delete a cohort, its people have no cohort, but their data stays.
- An organisation can be deleted only when it has no cohorts, no people and no country mapped to it.
- A partner admin can delete a cohort only when it holds nobody but the people they see. Otherwise the screen says only DIESIS can delete it.

### Add a person

1. Open **People & roles** (the page is called **People**).
2. Under **Add someone to the project**, enter the **Email** and, if you like, a **Name**.
3. Choose the **Role**. The form starts on **Learner**, so change it before you add staff. A partner admin can choose **Learner** or **Facilitator**.
4. **Admins** choose the **Organisation** (**— none (SENIC staff) —** for SENIC). A partner admin's people always join their own organisation.
5. **For a learner under 16**, tick **Under 16: our organisation holds a parent's or guardian's permission** only if your organisation really holds that permission, on paper or by email. If the permission also covers sharing the learner's Comp Card with your facilitators, tick that too.
6. Leave **Send them an invitation email** ticked and choose the language: English, Deutsch or Ελληνικά. It starts on your own screen language.
7. Press **Add**.

The invitation names you and their role, lists their workspaces and has the one-click link.

- Adding someone who is already on the list gives them the new role. It takes nothing away, and it never moves them to another organisation: to move someone, an admin uses **Change details**.
- Someone who has signed in before gets no invitation and needs none. They see the new workspace next time.
- "This address can't be added from here. Ask DIESIS." means the address belongs to someone your organisation can't add: a learner of another organisation, or an account that already has learning in it.
- If a person your organisation added gives a country that belongs to another organisation, they leave your organisation when they finish **Before you start**, and DIESIS checks where they belong.

### Change details

Press **Change details** in a person's row.

- **Admins** change the name, the country and the organisation. Choosing a country suggests its organisation; you can keep another one before you save.
- **Partner admins** change the name of their own learners only.

Moving a learner to another organisation:
- sets their Comp Card back to "Only me";
- removes a guardian's permission recorded by the old organisation. The row then says "guardian's permission not recorded here". The new organisation records its own by adding the learner again with the box ticked.

### Lists for admins

Admins choose a list under **Show**: **People with a role**; **No role: removed, or never finished signing up**; **No organisation**; **Country doesn't match organisation**; and **Added by a partner admin, never signed in**.

### Invite again

**Status** shows **Joined** once someone has signed in. Until then it shows **Invited** with a date, or **Not joined yet**.

1. At the top, tick **Send them an invitation email** and choose the language. The new invitation uses that language.
2. In the person's row, press **Resend invitation** (or **Send invitation**).

Each address can get up to 5 invitations an hour. If sending fails, the message says why.

### Change a role

In the **Roles** column, press a role to switch it on or off. The change saves at once.

- For an admin, **Editor** shows as "included with Admin". For a partner admin, **Editor** and **Facilitator** are included with **Partner admin**. You can't switch an included role on or off by itself.
- Everyone keeps at least one role. To turn an admin into an editor, first add them again with the role **Editor** (invitation unticked). Then switch off **Admin**.
- On your own row, **Admin** stays on: you can't switch off your own Admin role. Another admin can.
- Staff who signed in before 13 October may still show **Learner**. This does no harm, and you can switch it off.

### Assign a cohort

Choose a cohort in the person's **Cohort** column. Cohorts are shown as "short name · cohort", and only the cohorts of the person's own organisation are offered. Choosing a cohort never moves someone to another organisation. **— none —** clears the cohort.

### Remove someone

Press the bin icon at the end of a row. The page asks first and says what will happen. Press **Remove from project** to go ahead, or **Keep them**. Once you have pressed Remove, it can't be stopped. You can't remove yourself. Another admin can.

**Remove** takes away all their roles and their cohort, and they disappear from the list until their next page. It does **not**:
- delete their account, progress, Comp Card, or notes about them;
- sign them out or stop them signing in. On their next page they get the **Learner** role and show up as a learner.

So Remove doesn't stop their data being used, and it is no answer to a request to restrict it: see the data-management guide (`docs/data-management.md`, §3).

To erase an account, the person can use **Delete my account** on their account page. Or ask DIESIS: an admin erases it on the **Data** page.

### Admins named in the server setting

A few admins are named in a server setting that SENIC manages. They always have Admin and Editor. If you switch these roles off or remove the person, the roles come back on their next page. The screen doesn't show who these admins are. Ask SENIC to change the list.

### Before partner staff get Facilitator or Partner admin

An admin first links them to their organisation (**Change details**), and gives the role only from Wednesday 25 November, once the security check has passed (*Whose learners you see*).

## Support learners

**Support learners** opens the **Facilitator workspace**. It lists the learners you may see (*Whose learners you see*), whether they have started or not. Staff are never listed. A learner can carry a label:
- **Test account**: an account an admin marked as a test (*Test accounts*);
- **Former staff**: someone who held a staff role before.

### The figures at the top

- **Cohort completion**: the average progress of all learners in the list, and how many finished every course they started. Learners who haven't started count as 0%.
- **Active in the last 24h**: until 10 November, despite its name, this counts learners who have made any progress at any time. To see recent activity, use **Last active** or Statistics. **From 10 November**: learners whose **Last active** is within the 24 hours before you opened the page. A learner who opened a course but saved nothing new isn't counted; Statistics, which also counts opening a course, can show them as seen more recently.
- **Comp Cards shared with you**: learners who have shared their Comp Card with your organisation.
- **Needs attention**: learners who have started, are below 50% and haven't finished. It doesn't look at when they were last active.

Press **Open learner** on a row to see details.

### What you see per learner

- Name, email, and progress across the courses they started.
- **Activity & performance**: **Currently on** (the next lesson in their latest course), **Time on task**, **Last active**, and time per lesson.
  - **From 10 November**, **Currently on** shows the name the course gives that step, following the course's own order and its own lessons, and "—" when there is no lesson to name. Time per lesson lists the seven standard lessons, plus **Other lessons** for time spent in lessons a course added of its own, so the rows add up to the time on task.
- Results as right/total: **Assessment** (quick checks), **Practice** (guided practice) and **Video** (questions inside videos).
- **Choice in the applied scenario**: the choice they made in the course's scenario, under Activity.
- Their **Comp Card**, if they shared it with your organisation (below). You don't see their answers to reflection questions in courses.

Learners are told that, whatever they choose for their Comp Card, their organisation's facilitators always see their name and email, country, progress, last activity, time per lesson, quiz and video-question results, the choices they make in exercises, and whether a guardian's permission is recorded.

### The Comp Card

From 20 November, a learner's Comp Card is private unless the learner shares it.

- A learner chooses **Only me** (the default) or **Me and the facilitators at ‹organisation›**. On 20 November every card goes back to **Only me**: learners who want to share choose again.
- If the card is shared with your organisation, you see all of it. If not, you see "Nothing shared with you."
- A learner can switch back to **Only me** at any time, from the Comp Card or their account page. If a learner moves to another organisation, their card goes back to **Only me**.
- A learner under 16 who was invited with a guardian's permission can share only if their organisation recorded that the permission covers sharing.
- SENIC's admins see no Comp Card text. A DIESIS admin sees the cards shared with DIESIS.
- There is no mentor option any more.

### Write a note or validate a competence

1. Open the learner.
2. Write in **Observation note** (up to 2,000 characters). Describe what the learner did, not what kind of person they are.
3. If you saw the competence in practice, tick **Validate this evidence**. Your note becomes the evidence, and **Skill area** appears. A note on its own has no skill area.
4. Check the **Skill area**. It starts on the skill area of the learner's current course. Choose another if that is what you saw.
5. Press **Save**.

- You can write notes only about learners you see.
- Each time you press Save, a new entry is added. You can't read, edit or delete notes on screen afterwards.
- You can't change a validation's skill area after you save. If you choose another skill area and save again, a second validation is added and the first one stays. The screen says so before you save.
- Learners don't see notes in the platform, but the notes are in the file they get from **Download my data**, signed "a facilitator at ‹your organisation›", never with your name. Write as if they will read it.
- If the learner hasn't started a course, or their course's skill area isn't one of the platform's seven, choose the skill area yourself before you save.
- To correct or delete a note or a validation, contact DIESIS.

## Statistics

**Statistics** opens **Learner statistics**, which is in English only.

**Who counts, from 20 November:** learners who have started a course **since 1 December 2026**. Nobody who holds or ever held a staff role, and no account marked as a test account, is counted. Partner staff see their own organisation's learners only. The page says what it counts: "Counting from 1 December 2026. Staff, former staff and test accounts are left out." Until then it says "Counting starts on 1 December 2026." Each learner is measured on the course they used most recently.

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Learners | Learners who have started a course |
| Active this week | Of those, active in the last 7 days |
| Completed | Finished their most recent course |
| Avg progress | Average progress through that course |
| Avg time | Average active minutes per learner, all courses |
| Badges | Badges earned in total |

The charts use the standard lesson names. "Simulation" is guided practice, and "Assessment" is the quick check.

**From 10 November**, a learner who is on one of a course's own lessons is counted in an extra **Other lessons** bar of the funnel and shows as "Other lesson" under **Currently on**; time in such lessons is an **Other lessons** column in **Time per lesson**. The funnel still counts them on the standard lessons they finished. Figures for each lesson of a course's own come later.

- **Course progress funnel**: how many learners reached each of the seven standard lessons, and how many finished. "biggest drop-off" marks the step where the number falls most from the step before.
- **Where they get stuck**: that drop-off, the slowest lesson, and how many learners are part-way through a course and haven't been active for 3 days or more.
- **Time per lesson**: average active time per lesson. Time when the browser tab is hidden doesn't count.
- **Activity (last 14 days)**: how many times courses were opened each day. It counts openings, not people.
- **When they open**: course openings by weekday, in 4-hour blocks. Times are UTC. In winter (from 25 October), UTC is one hour behind Germany and Belgium and two hours behind Greece and Cyprus.
- **Each learner**: name, cohort, **Currently on**, progress, time, quiz score, **Last seen**, and a status of **Active**, **Stuck** (not active for 3 days or more) or **Completed**.

If the page is empty, it says why: no learner has started yet, counting hasn't started, your account isn't linked to an organisation, or the figures couldn't be read (reload in a few minutes).

## Test accounts

Use a test learner account only to see what learners see.

1. Keep a list of your test addresses and send it to DIESIS.
2. An admin marks each one as a test account on **Admin → Data**.

A marked account:
- is left out of Statistics at once, and shows **Test account** on Support learners;
- can be reset by an admin: its progress, Comp Card, badges and the notes about it go, and the account and its answers to *Before you start* stay;
- is erased in the go-live clean-up on 30 November.

Partner admins can't mark, reset or erase accounts.

## The Data page (admins only)

**Admin → Data** is for DIESIS's and SENIC's admins. It is where they find anyone by email address, download a copy of someone's data or erase their account for a request in DIESIS's register (reference `REQ-YYYY-NNN`), mark and reset test accounts, check the review lists, run the go-live clean-up and read the log. The steps are in the data-management guide.

Partner admins and facilitators have no data tools: pass any data request you receive to DIESIS.

## Safeguarding contacts

Learners see, on their pages:
- a **Need to talk to someone?** box with 112 and the contacts for their country;
- a **Need help?** link in the footer of every page, to the help page with every country's contacts;
- a line beside every reflection: "Facilitators don't read what you write here.", with a link to help, and a "Get help" line on the Comp Card.

The contacts come from **Admin → Safeguarding contacts**, where an admin enters up to three per country: the partner organisation's contact and help lines. Use a role or a team mailbox, not a person's name.

- **Partners:** send DIESIS your organisation's safeguarding contact (role, email address or phone number, hours) and a national help line, by 20 November.
- Until a country's contacts are in, learners there see 112 and "Talk to someone you trust at your partner organisation."
- The platform alerts nobody. Facilitators don't read reflections. If a learner tells you something worrying, follow your organisation's safeguarding policy.

## German and Greek

From 20 November, each course you create has a German and a Greek switch on its course page in **Manage courses**.

- Until a language is switched on, learners using German or Greek get the course in English, with a note saying so.
- A partner admin (or an admin) switches a language on once its translation is checked: Pro Arbeit for German, Synthesis or University of Macedonia for Greek. Until partner admins are invited on 25 November, ask DIESIS or SENIC in writing to switch it on for you.
- Built-in courses are always in all three languages.
- Write the English first: a course you create can't be published for the first time until its English is complete.
- The *Lesson editor partner guide* has the details (*Switching German or Greek on*).

Staff screens other than Support learners stay in English.

## A learner asks to change their name or country

Learners can't change their name or country themselves. Their account page tells them to ask.

1. Pass the request to DIESIS by email: [DIESIS contact address]. Say:
   - the learner's email address as it is in the platform;
   - what they want changed: the new name, or the new country;
   - that the learner asked for it, and when.
   Don't send anything else about the learner (no progress, no notes, no screenshots), and don't ask them for an identity document.
2. DIESIS records the request in its register and decides.
3. An admin makes the change on **Manage people** (**Change details**).

If the new country belongs to another organisation, the learner moves there:
- their Comp Card goes back to "Only me";
- a guardian's permission your organisation recorded goes with your organisation;
- your organisation no longer sees them.

Nobody can change an email address in the platform: the learner can delete their account and sign up again with the new address.

A partner admin can correct a typing mistake in the name of a learner they added (**Change details**). A learner's own request goes to DIESIS.

## My learning for staff

Every staff member has **My learning**, with courses, badges and a Comp Card saved to your account. Since 13 October, your progress doesn't appear in Support learners or Statistics, and nobody can write notes about you. From 20 November, Statistics never count anyone who has held a staff role, even after their staff roles are removed.

**Your account** (the person icon in the top bar) has **Download my data** and **Delete my account**. If you delete a staff account, the notes you wrote stay, without your name.

## What changes on 10 November (Release 2)

These apply from the Release 2 deploy, planned for Tue 10 November 2026.

- **Courses as built:** learners get a created course as its editors built it: its own lessons and step names, its order, the content added to lessons. Hidden lessons are left out. **Preview as learner** shows exactly this.
- **Progress** counts each course's lessons as learners get them. A finished lesson that is later hidden still counts, so hiding never lowers anyone's progress. A finished course stays at 100%.
- **Learners carry on where they stopped:** a signed-in learner opens a course at the first lesson they haven't done, with their practice and scenario answers kept.
- **Support learners and Statistics:** the new meaning of **Active in the last 24h**, step names under **Currently on**, and **Other lessons** for the lessons a course added of its own, as described above.
- **Courses with learners:** an editor confirms changes to a course people are taking, and from 1 December its lessons and content can no longer be added, moved or removed. The *Lesson editor partner guide* has the details.

## What changes on 20 November (Release 3)

These apply from the Release 3 deploy on Fri 20 November 2026.

- **Before you start:** everyone who signs in answers a one-time screen: first name or nickname, country, Terms of use, age. Learners get the Learner role only once they have answered it.
- **Partner admin:** a new role for each partner organisation (*The partner admin role*).
- **Own organisation only:** partner admins and facilitators see only their own organisation's learners, from the security check on 23–25 November (*Whose learners you see*). Admins set the country mapping and people's organisations.
- **Comp Card:** private unless the learner shares it with their organisation's facilitators; every card starts as "Only me" again.
- **Statistics:** counting starts on 1 December; staff, former staff and test accounts are left out.
- **Test accounts:** admins mark, reset and erase them; marked accounts are erased on 30 November.
- **The Data page:** admins handle data requests and the go-live clean-up there.
- **Safeguarding:** contacts per country, the help page and a help line beside reflections.
- **German and Greek:** a switch per course; learners get English until it is on.
- **Videos:** a YouTube video loads only when the learner presses play. Videos, captions and documents come from the platform's own storage.

## Good practice with learners' data

- **Practise on drafts and test accounts.** Use **Preview as learner** on a draft course, where nothing is saved, or use **My learning** on your staff account. Use a test learner account only to see what learners see, and have it marked as a test account (*Test accounts*).
- **Look only at what you need** for supporting your learners.
- **Don't copy learner data out of the platform.** No screenshots, spreadsheets or emails with names, progress, Comp Card answers or notes.
- **Write notes with care.** Leave out health or family details.
- **Send data requests to DIESIS**: [DIESIS contact address]. This covers a request to see, correct or delete data, a wrong name or note, a role given by mistake, or an account to erase. Learners can download or delete their own data from their account page.
